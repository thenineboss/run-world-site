Privacy policy

Effective 27 October 2026

Run World ("we", "us") is a running app for iPhone and Android. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

Some settings stay only on your phone and are never sent to us: your weight (for calorie estimates), weekly goal, training plan progress, voice settings and day or night look.

Location

Run World uses your location only while you are recording a run or a hike, including when the screen is locked so the run keeps measuring. It does not use your location at any other time. Your exact location is never shown to other users, except to the safety contacts you choose, during a run.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores your account and app data. Servers may be outside your country.
  • Expo, Apple and Google deliver push notifications.
  • Apple Maps shows maps, and your phone's built-in place search finds places when you plan a journey.

These services process data only to provide their part of the app.

How long we keep it

We keep your data until you delete your account. Live locations are replaced at each run, and voice cheers are deleted once played. Reports about abusive content may be kept for up to one year to protect the community.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Profile → Delete my account. This immediately and permanently deletes your login, runs, routes, photos, messages, friends, group memberships and everything else linked to your account. If you can no longer open the app, email us from the address you signed up with at thenineboss@gmail.com and we will delete it within 30 days.

Your rights

You can see and change your profile in the app, and download or delete your data by contacting us. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Malaysia's PDPA), you may have further rights, such as to object to processing or to complain to a data protection authority.

Children

Run World is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
